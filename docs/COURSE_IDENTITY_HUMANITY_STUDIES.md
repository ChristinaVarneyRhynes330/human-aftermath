# Humanity Studies: The Wound Concealed
## Course Identity and Phase 1 Editorial Design System

**Status:** Proposed course brand implemented on review branch; requires creator's visual approval before production release.
**Not the academy name.** The academy's name has not yet been selected.

### Identity
- **Signature course:** Humanity Studies: The Wound Concealed
- **Course statement:** Trauma Literacy. Transforming Humanity.
- **Guiding philosophy:** Understand More. Assume Less.
- **Learning transformation:** From trauma literacy to human literacy.
- **Signature reasoning:** Observation is not explanation. No single lens tells the whole story.

### Meaning of the title
*The Wound Concealed* describes psychological pain formed internally through lived experience; unlike a physical wound, it cannot be identified and tended with ordinary physical wound care, dressing or stitches. The phrase names an experience that may be unseen, **not a license to assume everyone is wounded or every behavior reflects trauma.** The course teaches both recognition of unseen influences **and** uncertainty, evidence, boundaries, safety, and accountability.

### Course vs. academy
**The course** is an eight-module, 65-lesson curriculum about trauma, adaptation, memory, development, evidence and humane interpretation. In Phase 1 this curriculum is a preview; full enrollment is not available.

**The academy** is a distinct future ambition: interdisciplinary educational pathways in human behavior, relationships, perception, communication, development and institutional systems. It has no final name and must never be presented as currently accredited, established, or offering unspecified courses.

### Visual identity source
Adapted from the supplied **The Human Aftermath™ Brand Style Guide** (its visual rationale, not its retired working title). The foundational motif is **Layered Understanding**: different lenses and contexts become legible in relationship to one another.

**Approved visual palette from original guide**
- Ink \`#172126\` — primary type, closing sections
- Deep Evergreen \`#29433C\` — primary brand / high-weight sections, buttons
- Weathered Sage \`#7D9286\` — quiet structural layers
- Warm Stone \`#C9C1B5\` — boundaries, infographics, secondary layers
- Bone \`#F4F0E9\` — primary light reading canvas
- Muted Clay \`#A86E5D\` — relationship/human accent, not danger
- Soft Gold \`#C0A56A\` — about 5% or less, fine contours and integration marks

**Rough visual balance:** Bone 55%, Ink/Evergreen 25%, Sage/Stone 15%, Clay/Gold 5%. Never introduce neon, rainbow gradients, wellness-pink overload, or clinical-blue dominance.

**Typography:** Sophisticated editorial serif for display; legible humanist sans for navigation/body. Generous whitespace, quiet outlines, comfortable line height. Avoid ornamental script, bubbly buttons, deep shadows.

**Artwork:** No crying stock photo, portrait of suffering, brain-in-a-globe graphics, wounds/bandages, butterflies, broken glass or puzzle pieces. Use **imperfect open contours, topographic mapping, eight-lens context relationships, thresholds, pathways and connecting nodes**. The Phase 1 graphic is the original inline SVG component \`InnerExperienceArt\` (abstract map).

**Accessibility:** Visible focus states, text alternatives for diagrams, no color-only meaning, no flashy animation, print-friendly starter guide.
